# TikTok Ads Audit Checklist

## Account

- advertiser enabled
- currency/timezone known
- role/permission enough for reads
- active/inactive campaign count

## Measurement

- conversion objective defined
- Pixel/Event source exists
- recent event activity checked
- attribution/conversion lag considered
- zero conversion interpreted with caution

## Structure

- objective matches funnel
- campaign naming useful
- adgroup segmentation deliberate
- ad count enough for test but not fragmented

## Creative

- enough ad-level report data
- hook/retention/click signal reviewed
- CTA matches objective
- destination page reviewed if available

## Budget

- spend enough for conclusion
- daily/lifetime budget mode understood
- pacing concentration by day checked
- budget changes reversible

## Policy

- rejected/limited delivery statuses checked
- claims, regulated categories, and landing compliance considered
